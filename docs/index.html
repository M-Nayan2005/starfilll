Privacy Policy – StarFill: Feedback Form Rater

Last updated: October 2026

StarFill does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any data.

  • It requests no browser permissions (no storage, tabs, history, cookies, or identity access).
  • A small script runs on web pages so it can notice when a page contains several star-rating fields. This check happens entirely on your device; page content is never copied, saved, or sent anywhere.
  • It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, ads, or tracking.
  • It never reads passwords, form text, or personal details, and it never submits a form for you.
  • Nothing is remembered between pages or visits.
